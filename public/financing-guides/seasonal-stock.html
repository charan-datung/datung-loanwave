How can I finance extra stock for the Christmas season?

A business loan or a revolving credit line can pay for stock before your busy season, and you repay from the season's sales. Datung offers both to established Philippine suppliers and distributors, for 3 to 12 months. Subject to credit approval.

An approved limit you can draw from, repay and draw again whenever the business needs it.

It suits FMCG distributors, general traders and retail suppliers stocking up for Christmas or school opening.

How it works with Datung

  • Apply once, and we review your business to set an approved limit.
  • Draw from your limit when the business needs it: for stock, a big order or a busy season.
  • Repay on the schedule set out in your documents.
  • As you repay, draw again from your limit whenever you need to.

What we look at

  • How your sales and cash flow move month to month, from your bank statements
  • How often you restock, and from whom
  • Your customers and the terms you give them
  • What our credit officer sees at the business visit

What you need to apply

To apply for a business loan, you need 1 valid ID and your last 3 months of bank or GCash statements.

Business loan pre-approval is usually within about 1 hour of complete documents, during business hours. This is an aim, not a guarantee.
