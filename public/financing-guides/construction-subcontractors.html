How can a construction subcontractor or supplier get financing?

Subcontractors and construction suppliers often wait on billings and post-dated checks. PO financing, invoice factoring and check rediscounting can turn those into working capital. Datung offers all three to established businesses. Subject to credit approval.

Financing that can fit

PO financing

Funds to buy the goods for a confirmed customer order, so you can say yes to the big one.

Invoice factoring

Get cash for invoices you've already issued, instead of waiting for your customers to pay.

Check rediscounting

Turn your customers' post-dated checks into cash now, instead of waiting for them to clear.

What we look at

  • Your latest bank statements and how cash moves through the business
  • What the money is for, and how it helps the business earn
  • Whether the repayments fit your cash flow
  • What our credit officer sees at the business visit
