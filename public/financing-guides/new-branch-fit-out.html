How can I finance a new branch, fit-out or renovation?

A business loan can pay for the costs of opening or improving a site, such as the deposit, fit-out, renovation or opening stock, and is repaid over 3 to 12 months. Because the term is short, it suits plans your existing business can repay from its current sales. Datung offers business loans of ₱30,000 to ₱10,000,000 to established Philippine businesses. What we look at

  • Your latest bank statements and how cash moves through the business

What you need to apply

To apply for a business loan, you need 1 valid ID and your last 3 months of bank or GCash statements.

Business loan pre-approval is usually within about 1 hour of complete documents, during business hours. This is an aim, not a guarantee.
