How can I fund a purchase order in the Philippines?

With PO financing: a lender provides funds to buy the goods for a confirmed customer order, and you repay on an agreed schedule. Datung offers PO financing to established Philippine suppliers and distributors. Subject to credit approval.

Funds to buy the goods for a confirmed customer order, so you can say yes to the big one.

How it works with Datung

  • Your customer issues a confirmed purchase order.
  • You apply with Datung and show us the purchase order during review.
  • If approved, the funds help you buy the goods and deliver the order.
  • You repay on the schedule set out in your loan documents, which you see in full before you sign.

What we look at

  • The confirmed purchase order, and the customer who issued it
  • Your track record of delivering similar orders
  • Your latest bank statements and how cash moves through the business
  • What our credit officer sees at the business visit

What you need to apply

To apply for a business loan, you need 1 valid ID and your last 3 months of bank or GCash statements.

Business loan pre-approval is usually within about 1 hour of complete documents, during business hours. This is an aim, not a guarantee.
