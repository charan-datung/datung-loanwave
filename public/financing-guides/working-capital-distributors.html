What working capital financing suits a distributor?

It depends on how your cash moves. Distributors that restock often may suit a revolving credit line: draw when you need stock, repay, and draw again. If you need one lump sum, a business loan may fit better. Datung offers both, of ₱30,000 to ₱10,000,000. Subject to credit approval.

Questions

Which loan is best for working capital?

It depends on the need. A credit line suits repeat needs, a business loan suits a single need, and PO financing or invoice factoring suit cash tied to one order or invoice.

Can I get a loan for working capital?

Owners of established businesses in any industry can apply, especially suppliers and distributors who sell to small businesses on terms. Subject to credit approval.
