Can my business borrow to pay 13th month pay?

Yes. An established business can use a business loan for payroll, including 13th month pay, and repay over 3 to 12 months. Datung offers business loans of ₱30,000 to ₱10,000,000. Subject to credit approval.

A lump sum for inventory, equipment, payroll or your peak season, repaid over 3 to 12 months.

How it works with Datung

  • Apply once, online, and tell us what the money is for.
  • We review your application and visit your business.
  • If approved, you sign your loan documents and receive your funds.
  • Repay over your agreed term. Tell us whether you'd prefer weekly, bi-weekly or monthly payments.

What we look at

  • Your latest bank statements and how cash moves through the business
  • What the money is for, and how it helps the business earn
  • Whether the repayments fit your cash flow
  • What our credit officer sees at the business visit

What you need to apply

To apply for a business loan, you need 1 valid ID and your last 3 months of bank or GCash statements.

Business loan pre-approval is usually within about 1 hour of complete documents, during business hours. This is an aim, not a guarantee.
