What is Datung?

Datung is the business name of Dark Knight Lending, Inc., a lending company registered with the Philippine Securities and Exchange Commission and a subsidiary of Datung Pte. Ltd., Singapore.

It offers PO financing, invoice factoring, check rediscounting, revolving credit lines, collateral-based financing and business loans of ₱30,000 to ₱10,000,000, for 3 to 12 months, to established Philippine SMEs, especially the suppliers and distributors who sell to small businesses on terms. Subject to credit approval.

Datung's main business is business loans and working capital for established Philippine SMEs, especially suppliers and distributors. It also offers salary loans for employees and group micro loans for micro-entrepreneurs.
