Datung

For institutional funders

Datung is the business name of Dark Knight Lending, Inc., a Philippine lending company licensed by the Securities and Exchange Commission. We lend to established Philippine SMEs, especially the suppliers and distributors who sell to small businesses on terms.

We work with a small number of institutional funders: banks, private credit funds, family offices and development finance institutions.

What does Datung lend?

PO financing, invoice factoring, check rediscounting, revolving credit lines, collateral-based financing and business loans of ₱30,000 to ₱10,000,000, for 3 to 12 months. We also offer salary loans and group loans.

Subject to credit approval.

How are loans approved?

An applicant sends an application, a valid government ID and their latest 3 months of bank or GCash statements. A credit officer reviews them. After pre-approval we check trade references and visit the business. No final decision is made before the visit.

How are decisions and money controlled?

  • Decision log. Credit, money, policy and AI decisions go into an append-only, hash-chained log that nobody can edit or delete.
  • Separation of duties. Each loan release and payment reversal records who approved and who acted. If the same person did both, that is written to the decision log as an exception.
  • Daily reconciliation. An internal reconciliation runs every day, and every exception goes to the decision log.
